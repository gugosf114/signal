# Does the score mean anything? First measurement, 2026-09-06

The 0–100 "market pressure" number had never been checked against a price.
TCGplayer's per-product price history opened that door tonight (see
`src/services/priceHistory.js`). This is the first look. It is a nowcast, not
a forecast: the 30-day move measured here is the move that happened **before**
each scan. The forward test starts from the same rows in
`scripts/backtest-baseline.json` and reads in 30 days with
`python3 scripts/backtest.py check`.

## Basket

68 exact printings from TCGplayer's weekly "Biggest Price Spikes", "Biggest
Price Drops" and "Movers and Shakers" articles (Pokémon 27, Magic 37,
Yu-Gi-Oh 5), resolved through the app's own resolver, scanned through the live
pipeline (Haiku, one direct search, all pre-fetch lanes) on 2026-09-06 between
23:05 and 23:15 PT. 55 had an exact TCGplayer SKU history. One scan failed
because the model refused to output JSON; the prompt now forbids that.
Raw rows: `scripts/basket-2026-09-06.json`. Script: `scripts/fit_weights.py`.

Two known biases, stated up front: the articles select cards that already
moved (36 rose, 19 fell), and the YouTube quota ran out halfway, so the later
(mostly Magic) cards had no creator lane.

## Result

| measure | value |
|---|---|
| score, mean / sd / range | 55.2 / 9.1 / 47–89 |
| scans that landed on exactly 50 | 24 of 55 |
| Spearman(score, 30-day move) | −0.27 |
| AUC(score → price rose) | 0.42 (0.5 is a coin flip) |
| cards scored > 60 | median move −1.9%, 5 of 11 rose |
| cards scored exactly 50 | median move +39.9%, 17 of 24 rose |

| logistic fit on the 8 signal contributions, in-sample AUC | 0.73 |
| same fit, leave-one-out AUC | 0.52 |

## What it says

1. **The score did not describe the past month.** High scores went with flat
   or falling prices; the flat 50s were the cards that had jumped. Cards that
   quadrupled (Bloatfly Swarm +422%, Knucklebone Witch +494%, Glóin +388%)
   scored 47–50 with almost no evidence.
2. **Evidence follows price.** The more a card had already moved and settled,
   the more creators and articles talked about it, and the more evidence the
   app found. Attention is a lagging indicator here, not a leading one.
3. **Nothing learnable in 55 rows.** The fit looks good in-sample and is a
   coin flip out of sample, and it flips the sign of three signals. No weight
   change is shipped from this.

## What to do with it (George decides)

- Show the real move first. History is live now; the number the reader can
  check (30-day, 90-day, copies sold) belongs above a number nobody can.
- Rename what the score measures. It reads attention, and the app should say
  attention until the forward test says otherwise.
- Read the forward test on 2026-10-06. Same 68 rows, exact SKUs, one command.
